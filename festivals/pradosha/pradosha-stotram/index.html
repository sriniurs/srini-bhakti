WHERE WE ARE

The Skanda Purāṇa gives Pradoṣa its own hymn: the Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam. It opens with a solemn declaration, paints the scene of Śiva’s twilight dance on Kailāsa with the gods as his orchestra, and closes with the sage Śāṇḍilya’s words from the vrata story (Part 3).

Here is every verse in IAST, with its meaning, key words and a note. Read it slowly at twilight on the next Trayodaśī.

ZAPPY: Eight verses or nine?

PROF. BOLT: Seven of praise and two of the story in this edition. The title says “eight”; editions differ.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 1

THE ONE ESSENTIAL THING
satyaṃ bravīmi paralokahitaṃ bravīmisāraṃ bravīmyupaniṣaddhṛdayaṃ bravīmi .saṃsāramulbaṇamasāramavāpya jantoḥsāro’yamīśvarapadāmburuhasya sevā .. 1..
I speak the truth, I speak what is good for the next world, I speak the essence, the heart of the Upaniṣads: for a creature caught in this harsh, worthless round of births, the one thing of value is service to the lotus feet of the Lord.
WordMeaning
satyam bravīmiI speak the truth
upaniṣad-hṛdayamthe heart of the Upaniṣads
saṃsāram asāramthe worthless round of births
īśvara-pada-amburuhathe Lord’s lotus feet
sevāservice

The real promise first. Before any talk of wealth, the hymn states its core: serving the Lord is the one essential thing.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
The one thing that matters: service at Śiva’s feet.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 2

THOSE WHO NEGLECT IT
ye nārcayanti giriśaṃ samaye pradoṣeye nārcitaṃ śivamapi praṇamanti cānye .etatkathāṃ śrutipuṭairna pibanti mūḍhāstejanmajanmasu bhavanti narā daridrāḥ .. 2..
Those who do not worship Giriśa at the time of Pradoṣa, who do not even bow to Śiva when others worship him, who do not drink this story with the cups of their ears: such deluded people are poor, birth after birth.
WordMeaning
girīśamŚiva, lord of the mountain
samaye pradoṣeat the time of Pradoṣa
śruti-puṭaiḥwith the cups of the ears
mūḍhāḥthe deluded
daridrāḥpoor

A warning. The verse is blunt, as Purāṇic praise often is. Its image is lovely: drinking a sacred story through the ears.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
Neglect the hour, stay poor.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 3

THOSE WHO KEEP IT
ye vai pradoṣasamaye parameśvarasya,kurvantyananyamanaso’ṅghrisarojapūjām .nityaṃ pravṛddhadhanadhānyakalatraputrasaubhāgya-sampadadhikāsta ihaiva loke .. 3..
Those who, at the time of Pradoṣa, worship the lotus feet of the Supreme Lord with undivided minds, grow ever richer here in this very world: in wealth, grain, wife, sons, good fortune and prosperity.
WordMeaning
ananya-manasaḥwith undivided minds
aṅghri-saroja-pūjāmworship of the lotus feet
pravṛddhaever increasing
saubhāgyagood fortune
iha eva lokehere in this world

Undivided. The condition is ananya-manas, a mind on nothing else. The fruits are worldly and frankly listed.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
Worship with an undivided mind: fortune here and now.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 4

THE DANCE ON KAILĀSA
kailāsaśailabhuvane trijagajjanitrīṃ gaurīṃniveśya kanakācitaratnapīṭhe .nṛtyaṃ vidhātumabhivāñchati śūlapāṇaudevāḥ pradoṣasamaye nu bhajanti sarve .. 4..
In his palace on Kailāsa, having seated Gaurī, mother of the three worlds, on a golden jewelled throne, when the trident-bearer wishes to dance, at the time of Pradoṣa, all the gods come to serve him.
WordMeaning
kailāsa-śaila-bhuvanein the palace on Mount Kailāsa
trijagaj-janitrīm gaurīmGaurī, mother of the three worlds
ratna-pīṭheon a jewelled throne
nṛtyam vidhātumto dance
śūla-pāṇauthe trident-bearer

Why the hour is holy. Śiva dances for Gaurī, and every god is present. Worship at this hour reaches all of them.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
At twilight Śiva dances, and all the gods attend.

VERSE 5

THE DIVINE ORCHESTRA
vāgdevī dhṛtavallakī śatamakho veṇuṃ dadhatpadmaja-stālonnidrakaro ramā bhagavatī geyaprayogānvitā .viṣṇuḥ sāndramṛdaṅgavādanapaṭurdevāḥ samantātsthitāḥsevante tamanu pradoṣasamaye devaṃ mṛḍānīpatim .. 5..
Sarasvatī holding the vīṇā, Indra bearing the flute, Brahmā with hands keeping time, the blessed Lakṣmī engaged in song, Viṣṇu skilled in playing the deep mṛdaṅga, the gods standing all around: they serve the Lord of Mṛḍānī at the time of Pradoṣa.
WordMeaning
vāgdevī dhṛta-vallakīSarasvatī holding the vīṇā
śatamakhaḥ veṇumIndra with the flute
padmajaḥ tālaBrahmā keeping time
ramā geyaLakṣmī singing
viṣṇuḥ mṛdaṅgaViṣṇu on the drum
mṛḍānī-patimthe husband of Mṛḍānī (Pārvatī)

A concert of the gods. The greatest deities become musicians for Śiva’s dance. Even Viṣṇu plays the drum.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
The gods as Śiva’s orchestra.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 6

EVERY BEING PRESENT
gandharvayakṣapatagoragasiddhasādhya-vidyādharāmaravarāpsarasāṃ gaṇāṃśca .ye’nye trilokanilayā sahabhūtavargāḥprāpte pradoṣasamaye harapārśvasaṃsthāḥ .. 6..
Gandharvas, yakṣas, birds, serpents, siddhas, sādhyas, vidyādharas, the best of the gods and the troops of apsarās, and all others who dwell in the three worlds, with the hosts of beings: when Pradoṣa comes, they stand beside Hara.
WordMeaning
gandharva … apsarasāmcelestials of every kind
triloka-nilayāḥdwellers of the three worlds
bhūta-vargāḥthe hosts of beings
prāpte pradoṣa-samayewhen Pradoṣa arrives
hara-pārśva-saṃsthāḥstanding at Hara’s side

Everyone. No being is left out of the twilight gathering.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
At Pradoṣa every being stands with Śiva.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 7

ŚIVA ALONE
ataḥ pradoṣe śiva eka evapūjyo’tha nānye haripadmajādyāḥ .tasminmaheśe vidhinejyamānesarve prasīdanti surādhināthāḥ .. 7..
Therefore at Pradoṣa Śiva alone is to be worshipped, not the others, Hari, Brahmā and the rest; when Maheśa is worshipped there according to rule, all the lords of the gods are pleased.
WordMeaning
ataḥtherefore
śiva eka eva pūjyaḥŚiva alone is to be worshipped
hari-padmaja-ādyāḥViṣṇu, Brahmā and the rest
vidhinā ijyamānebeing worshipped by rule
sarve prasīdantiall are pleased

Not rivalry. The point is not that Viṣṇu is lesser, but that at this hour all the gods are with Śiva: worship him and you worship them.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
Worship Śiva at Pradoṣa, and all the gods are pleased.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 8

THE SAGE TO THE WIDOW (1)
eṣa te tanayaḥ pūrvajanmani brāhmaṇottamaḥ .pratigrahairvayo ninye na dānādyaiḥ sukarmabhiḥ .. 8..
This son of yours was, in a former life, an excellent brahmin; he spent his life accepting gifts, not in giving and other good deeds.
WordMeaning
eṣa te tanayaḥthis son of yours
pūrva-janmaniin a former life
pratigrahaiḥby accepting gifts
na dāna-ādyaiḥnot by giving and the like

The story enters the hymn. These are Śāṇḍilya’s words to the widow about her son (Part 3).

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
A life of only taking brings poverty.

IN THE VERSE

VERSE 9

THE SAGE TO THE WIDOW (2)
ato dāridryamāpannaḥ putraste dvijabhāmini .taddoṣaparihārārthaṃ śaraṇāṃ yātu śaṅkaram .. 9..
That is why your son has fallen into poverty, O brahmin lady. To remove that fault, let him take refuge in Śaṅkara.
WordMeaning
ataḥ dāridryam āpannaḥso he has fallen into poverty
dvija-bhāminiO brahmin lady
tad-doṣa-parihāra-arthamto remove that fault
śaraṇam yātu śaṅkaramlet him take refuge in Śaṅkara

The remedy. Refuge in Śiva through Pradoṣa. The hymn ends where the vow begins.

Text: sanskritdocuments.org, Pradoṣa Stotrāṣṭakam (pradosha8), ITRANS converted to IAST; one typographic correction in verse 3.
The remedy: take refuge in Śaṅkara.

CHANTING IT AT PRADOṢA

Where the hymn fits

In the worship. The hymn is chanted during the Pradoṣa worship, often after the abhiṣeka and before the kathā.

With other hymns. Devotees also chant the Śiva Pañcākṣara Stotram, the Mahāmṛtyuñjaya mantra · 4 parts in 1 article and the Liṅgāṣṭakam.

Pronunciation. Our IAST guide explains the letters.

At home. If you cannot reach a temple, chant it before a lamp at sunset.
