PART 6 OF 8 · SEVEN DAYS · ŚANI PRADOṢA

The Seven Pradoṣas

Soma, Bhauma, Śani and the Rest · explained and illustrated

Seven planets, one twilight

HOW THIS WORKS: The kinds of Pradoṣa, then each weekday Pradoṣa from Sunday to Śani Pradoṣa, with what tradition says each brings.

WHERE WE ARE

A Pradoṣa takes extra colour from the weekday it falls on. Each day of the week belongs to a planet (graha), and tradition gives each weekday Pradoṣa its own fruit. Two stand out: Soma Pradoṣa (Monday) and above all Śani Pradoṣa (Saturday).

This part goes through all seven. The fruits listed are those of popular tradition and vrata books, not of a single scripture; we say so plainly.

ZAPPY: Why does the weekday matter?

PROF. BOLT: Two sacred times overlapping: Śiva’s twilight and a planet’s day. Devotees pray for what that planet rules.

KINDS OF PRADOṢA

Daily, fortnightly, monthly, great

Nitya. Some traditions count every twilight as a daily (nitya) Pradoṣa.

Pakṣa. Every Trayodaśī twilight: the fortnightly Pradoṣa, the usual vow.

Māsa. Some count the dark-fortnight Trayodaśī as the monthly Pradoṣa, leading into Māsa Śivarātri.

Mahā. A Pradoṣa falling on Saturday is called Mahā Pradoṣa; some lists add a pralaya Pradoṣa, the twilight at the end of time when all returns to Śiva.

ZAPPY: Every evening is a little Pradoṣa?

PROF. BOLT: In that sense, yes. Trayodaśī is the big one, Saturday the biggest.

Daily, fortnightly, monthly, great.

RAVI PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Sunday

The day. The Sun’s day. A Pradoṣa on Sunday is prayed on for health and vigour, joining Śiva’s grace with the Sun’s. Popular vrata books give it its own story of a devotee’s illness cured.

Prayed for. In popular tradition: health.

How. The same worship as every Pradoṣa (Part 5), with special attention on Sundays; some add prayers to the day’s planet.

Read honestly. These associations come from vrata books and astrological practice; the scriptural core is the same for every Pradoṣa.

ZAPPY: Is Sunday Pradoṣa better than others?

PROF. BOLT: Saturday is counted greatest, Monday next. But every Pradoṣa kept sincerely is complete.

Sunday Pradoṣa: health.

SOMA PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Monday

The day. The Moon’s day, and Monday is already Śiva’s, since he wears the moon. Soma Pradoṣa is one of the two most prized. Many recall the moon’s own cure by Śiva.

Prayed for. In popular tradition: peace of mind, the fulfilment of wishes.

How. The same worship as every Pradoṣa (Part 5), with special attention on Mondays; some add prayers to the day’s planet.

Read honestly. These associations come from vrata books and astrological practice; the scriptural core is the same for every Pradoṣa.

ZAPPY: Is Monday Pradoṣa better than others?

PROF. BOLT: Śiva’s own weekday at Śiva’s own hour. Many begin their Pradoṣa vow on a Monday.

Monday Pradoṣa: peace of mind, the fulfilment of wishes.

BHAUMA PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Tuesday

The day. Mars’s day (Bhauma, Maṅgala). A Tuesday Pradoṣa is prayed on for relief from debts and illness, and for strength; some also worship Hanumān.

Prayed for. In popular tradition: relief from debt and illness.

How. The same worship as every Pradoṣa (Part 5), with special attention on Tuesdays; some add prayers to the day’s planet.

Read honestly. These associations come from vrata books and astrological practice; the scriptural core is the same for every Pradoṣa.

ZAPPY: Is Tuesday Pradoṣa better than others?

PROF. BOLT: Saturday is counted greatest, Monday next. But every Pradoṣa kept sincerely is complete.

Tuesday Pradoṣa: relief from debt and illness.

BUDHA PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Wednesday

The day. Mercury’s day. Wednesday Pradoṣa is prayed on for intelligence, learning and success in work.

Wednesday Pradoṣa: learning, success.

GURU PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Thursday

The day. Jupiter’s day, the guru’s day. Thursday Pradoṣa is prayed on for wisdom, for one’s teacher, and against enemies; Dakṣiṇāmūrti is often worshipped.

Prayed for. In popular tradition: wisdom, the guru’s grace.

How. The same worship as every Pradoṣa (Part 5), with special attention on Thursdays; some add prayers to the day’s planet.

Read honestly. These associations come from vrata books and astrological practice; the scriptural core is the same for every Pradoṣa.

ŚUKRA PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Friday

The day. Venus’s day. Friday Pradoṣa is prayed on for marital harmony, prosperity and good fortune.

Prayed for. In popular tradition: harmony, prosperity.

How. The same worship as every Pradoṣa (Part 5), with special attention on Fridays; some add prayers to the day’s planet.

Read honestly. These associations come from vrata books and astrological practice; the scriptural core is the same for every Pradoṣa.

ŚANI PRADOṢA

Pradoṣa on a Saturday

The day. Saturn’s day. Śani Pradoṣa is the most prized of all, often called Mahā Pradoṣa. Those under a hard Saturn period (sāḍe-sāti) traditionally keep it, since Śiva is said to be Saturn’s own guru.

Prayed for. In popular tradition: release from Saturn’s afflictions, great merit.

How. The same worship as every Pradoṣa (Part 5), with special attention on Saturdays; some add prayers to the day’s planet.

Read honestly. These associations come from vrata books and astrological practice; the scriptural core is the same for every Pradoṣa.

ZAPPY: Is Saturday Pradoṣa better than others?

PROF. BOLT: The greatest of all, by tradition. Śiva is Saturn’s guru; at his twilight on Saturn’s day, Saturn himself is said to bow.

Saturday Pradoṣa: release from Saturn’s afflictions, great merit.
