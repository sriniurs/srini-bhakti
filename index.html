ABOUT BHAKTI · A PERSONAL FOREWORD

A Note from the Heart

The thought and devotion behind Bhakti

This little space was created with love for Maa Lalitā Tripurasundarī.

Whatever you find here is an offering at Her feet. It is through Her grace that I found the inspiration and opportunity to bring these articles together. I consider myself merely an instrument in Her hands.

Bhakti is a place to discover and feel closer to your Iṣṭa Devatā — through stories, sacred hymns, ślokas, and the wisdom behind them.

Many Names. One Divine.

For me, Śiva, Viṣṇu, Lalitā, Lakṣmī, Gaṇeśa, Hanumān and every other form of the Divine are One Supreme Reality, loved and called by different names.

I call that Supreme Maa Lalitā. You may call the same Supreme by a name dearer to your heart.

The names and forms may differ. The Divine is One. No path is greater or lesser.

Devotion Is Not a Transaction

I believe we should not worship merely for wealth, success, protection or rewards. Bhakti is not about what we receive. It is about love, trust and surrender.

A child does not love its mother for what she promises in return. Love itself is enough.

That is the spirit of this website. You will find explanations of hymns and their meanings, but not promises of the benefits of chanting them.

Read a śloka because it touches your heart. Learn about your Iṣṭa Devatā because you wish to feel closer to the Divine.

If these pages help even one person deepen that connection, this effort has served its purpose.

Whatever good comes of it, I offer back to Maa Lalitā.

May Her grace guide these words, and may every reader find their own path to the Divine.

With love and devotion,srini.presentsŚrī Mātre Namaḥ.
