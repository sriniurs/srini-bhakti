Śiva Mahimna Stotram

"Mahimnaḥ pāraṃ te…": the gandharva Puṣpadanta's great hymn to the glory of Śiva, explained verse by verse in four illustrated parts. Start with Part 1 and read in order.

  1. Part 1 · verses 1–11

    Praise Beyond Words

    1 article

    Even Brahmā's words fall short; the doubters answered; many rivers, one ocean; the pillar of fire.

    Read ↗
  2. Part 2 · verses 12–22

    The Great Deeds

    1 article

    Rāvaṇa under one toe, the blue throat, Kāma burnt, the dance, Tripura, Viṣṇu's eye and Dakṣa.

    Read ↗
  3. Part 3 · verses 23–32

    All and Beyond All

    1 article

    Ardhanārīśvara, the cremation ground, the yogī's bliss, the eight forms, Oṃ, and "if the ocean were ink".

    Read ↗
  4. Part 4 · verses 33–43

    The Closing Verses

    1 article

    Puṣpadanta named, the fruit of chanting, and "whatever You are, to That I bow".

    Read ↗
