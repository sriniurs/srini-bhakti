Śrīcakra · Āvaraṇa 8 of 9

Sarvasiddhiprada

The central triangle and Lalitā’s four emblems.

Where it sits

Sarvasiddhiprada is the eighth enclosure. Look for the single downward-pointing triangle inside the ring of eight. Its three corners belong to this enclosure. The point inside the triangle belongs to the ninth.

Presiding deity and attendants

Tripurāmbā is the presiding form of Lalitā, associated with the Atirahasya Yoginīs. The three corners are associated with Mahākāmeśvarī, Mahāvajreśvarī, and Mahābhagamālinī.

Bāṇinī, Cāpinī, Pāśinī, and Aṅkuśinī personify the arrows, bow, noose, and goad. These are the four emblems shown in Lalitā’s hands. The four personifications belong to the account of this enclosure alongside the three corner goddesses.

What it signifies

The name means “giver of all attainments.” The Bhāvanopaniṣad relates the corner goddesses to unmanifest nature, cosmic intelligence, and the sense of individuality. The triangle gives visible form to these three principles. The Khaḍgamālā and the Lalitopākhyāna offer complementary ways of reading the powers gathered around it.
