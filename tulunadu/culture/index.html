Tulu Nadu Culture

Yakṣagāna dance-drama, the Kambaḷa races of the paddy fields, and Durgā Namaskāra, the Goddess worshipped in a lamp.

← Tulu Nadu Temples Festivals Daiva & Nāga Traditions

  • Kambaḷa

    1 article

    Kambaḷa, the slush-track buffalo race of Tuḷunāḍu, as a rite: the pookare post, the four kinds of kambaḷa, the daivas of the field, the Koraga night before, the twin tracks and four race types, the care of the buffaloes, the runners, the court cases and the race today.

  • Yakṣagāna

    1 article

    Yakṣagāna, the night-long dance-drama of the coast: its history, the bhāgavata and the himmēḷa, the stage, the order of a night from the opening drums to maṅgaḷa, the plots, character types, costume and make-up, Teṅku and Baḍagu styles side by side, tāḷamaddaḷe and the temple troupes.

  • Durgā Namaskāra Pūjā

    1 article

    Durgā Namaskāra, the coast’s worship of the Goddess in a lamp: the lotus maṇḍala and the tall standing lamp, mātṛkā-nyāsa, the three Mothers, the Durgā Sūkta, how she got the name Durgā, the order of worship from saṅkalpa to the last bow, when it is done, and the homes and maṭhas that keep it.
