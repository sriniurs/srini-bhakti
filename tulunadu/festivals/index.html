Festivals of Tulu Nadu

The Tuḷu year, temple fairs, processions and seasonal observances.

← Tulu Nadu Temples Culture Daiva & Nāga Traditions

  • The Tuḷu year

    The twelve solar months, from Bisu to the harvest season.

  • Maṅgaḷūru Dasarā

    Navadurgā and Śāradā at Kudroḷi.

  • Uḍupi festivals

    Saptotsava, Kṛṣṇa’s birth and Viṭla Piṇḍi.

  • Lakṣa Dīpotsava

    The festival of lamps at Dharmasthaḷa.

  • Poḷali Jātre

    The month-long fair and the Poḷali Ceṇḍu ball game.

  • Kaṭīl festivals

    The island temple’s annual celebrations and fire ritual.

  • Koḍi Habba

    The festival and sugarcane chariot at Kōṭeśvara.

  • Bappanāḍu Jātre

    The annual fair and the great drums of Bappanāḍu.

  • Siri Jātre

    The shrine observances associated with the Siri epic.
