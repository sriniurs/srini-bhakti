Bhaja Govindam

"Bhaja govindaṃ, bhaja govindaṃ…": Ādi Śaṅkara's "hammer for delusion", sung to an old grammarian in Kāśī, explained verse by verse in three illustrated parts. Start with Part 1.

  1. Part 1 · verses 1–10

    Wake Up, O Mind

    1 article

    The old grammarian at Kāśī; wealth, the body, the drop on the lotus leaf, "who are you?", the ladder of good company.

    Read ↗
  2. Part 2 · verses 11–20

    The Noose of Desire

    1 article

    Time takes all; the robe and the belly; the old man and his stick; joy, joy, joy; a little Gītā, a drop of Gaṅgā.

    Read ↗
  3. Part 3 · verses 21–33

    The Way Across

    1 article

    Punarapi jananaṃ; one Viṣṇu in all; who am I?; the guru's feet; the grammarian awakened.

    Read ↗
