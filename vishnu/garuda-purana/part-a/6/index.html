GARUḌA PURĀṆA · PART A · 6 OF 8 · STARS, SIGNS, OMENS

READING THE WORLD

Islands and oceans, planets and palms, breath and omens

Ōṁ Namō Nārāyaṇāya

FOREWORD

Between the rites and the gems, the Ācāra Kāṇḍa packs in a whole course in reading the world: its shape, its peoples, the sky above it, and the signs it gives.

The earth is a ring of islands and seas around Mount Meru. The planets rule periods of a life. A sneeze, a bird at the left, a line on the palm or the nostril you are breathing through can all foretell success or failure.

Later chapters add grammar and metre, the tools of reading in the plainest sense.

Ōṁ Namō Nārāyaṇāya,
srini.presents 🙏

ZAPPY: Should we believe all of it?

PROF. BOLT: Read it as the knowledge system of its time. Some is cosmology, some is sky-watching, some is folk belief; all of it shows how people looked for order.

SEVEN ISLANDS, SEVEN SEAS

Chapter 54: the shape of the earth

Priyavrata’s gift. Manu’s son divides the earth into seven island-continents, one for each of seven sons.

The scale. The earth floats on water like a boat. Each ocean is twice as wide as the island it rings.

Beyond Jambu. In Plakṣa people do not die, and the outer islands know no division of ages.

MERU AND THE NINE LANDS

Chapters 54–55: inside Jambudvīpa

⛰️ Meru

A lakh of yōjanas across, 84,000 high, set on a base like the centre of a lotus.

🗺️ Nine lands

Ilāvṛta at the centre; Bhārata to the south; Kimpuruṣa also south of Meru. The grid is simplified.

BHĀRATA

Chapter 55: the land of action

⚖️ Its nature

In other lands perfection comes of itself; Bhārata is the one place it must be earned. This is why it is called the land of karma.

🧭 Its peoples

Kirātas to the east, Yavanas to the west, Āndhras to the south, Turuṣkas to the north; the four varṇas in the centre.

⛰️ Its mountains

Seven boundary ranges, among them Mahēndra, Malaya, Sahya and Vindhya.

🌊 Its rivers

Narmadā, Gōdāvarī, Kāvērī, Sarasvatī, Śatadru and many more, which wash away sin.

Regions. Pañcālas, Kurus and Matsyas in the middle; Magadhas and Vidēhas east; Draviḍas south; Kambōjas south-west in this listing.

PLANETARY PERIODS

Chapter 60: the daśās

Eight periods. Each planet rules a span of years in a life, in the order shown.

108. The eight add up to 108, the length of the eight-planet system that astrologers call aṣṭōttarī. Ketu has no period in it.

Rulerships. The chapter also assigns each zodiac sign its planet.

WHAT EACH PLANET BRINGS

During its period
☀️ Sunmisery and worry; a king may fall
🌙 Moonwealth, happiness, good food
☿ Mercuryprosperity, even a kingdom
🪐 Saturnthe ruin of kingdoms
♃ Jupiterkingdom, happiness, virtue
🌑 Rāhuruin, misery, disease
♀ Venuselephants, horses, kingdom, companions

Moon’s mansions. Chapter 61 goes star by star through the moon’s path; chapter 59 names the gods of planets and stars and where the Yōginīs stand on each day.

ZAPPY: Mars is missing from the table.

PROF. BOLT: The summary we have gives no fruit for Mars’s period, so it’s left out rather than guessed.

JOURNEYS AND SNEEZES

Everyday omens

🧳 Setting out

Some stars favour starting a journey; sights at the doorstep count as good or bad depending on whether they appear on the right or the left.

🤧 Sneezing

The meaning of a sneeze depends on the direction the person faces.

🧍 The Sun-figure

A human figure is divided among the stars: the star the Sun holds at birth, by its place on the body, foretells fortune; at the head, fine clothes; at the feet, a short life.

📿 A charm

A mantra written in sandal paste on birch bark, worn on neck or arm, wards off evil stars.

ZAPPY: Many families still pause after a sneeze.

PROF. BOLT: That custom is old and widespread; this chapter is one of its written sources.

THE EIGHT TREASURES

Chapter 53: Kubēra’s nidhis as kinds of people
Padmakindly; gathers gold and gives it to ascetics
Mahāpadmagives wealth to the pious
Makaracollects weapons, allies with kings, wins battles
Kacchapatrusts nobody; neither eats nor gives; hoards
Mukundawins kingdoms, spends freely on singers and dancers
Nandaloves praise and family; swaps old friends for new
Nīlastores cloth and grain; digs tanks for the public
Śaṅkhaspends on himself, leaves his kin poor

ZAPPY: A personality test from treasures?

PROF. BOLT: Eight ways of handling wealth. You can still spot every one of them.

MARKS OF THE BODY

Sāmudrika, chapters 63–65

🧑 Men

Chapter 63 reads fortune from a man’s build, limbs and features.

👩 Women

Chapter 64: curling hair, a round face and a deep navel mean a growing family; uneven hair and round eyes are read as widowhood.

✨ Oiliness

Oily eyes bring luck, oily teeth good food, oily skin a soft bed, oily feet a carriage.

📜 Both

Chapter 65 sets out the science of Sāmudrika as a whole.

Read as history. These judgements, especially on women, reflect their age, not any truth about people. They are recorded here as part of the text.

PALM LINES

From chapter 64

📏 Number

Too many lines mean misery; too few mean poverty.

🎨 Colour

Crimson lines bring happiness.

☸️ Signs

Fish, goad, lotus, discus or plough on palms and soles are auspicious; a gateway mark means a queen, even if born a servant.

💧 Condition

The good signs hold only if the palms do not sweat.

ŚĀLAGRĀMA STONES

Chapter 66

🪨 What they are

Black stones from the Gaṇḍakī river, worshipped as Viṣṇu himself.

🌀 Their marks

Markings are named for forms of Viṣṇu: Sudarśana, Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa, Acyuta, Caturbhuja, Vāsudēva, Ananta.

🙂 A surprise

The text prefers a plain stone, with no marks, to one with a circular mark.

🏠 Presence

Wherever a śālagrāma is kept, Viṣṇu dwells.

Also here. Names of good and bad years in the sixty-year cycle, and a time-reckoning called pañcasvara, based on five vowels.

CONQUEST OF BREATH

Chapter 67: Pavana Vijaya

🌙 Left breath

Jupiter, Venus, Mercury and the Moon. Good for trade, journeys, seeing a king, healing.

☀️ Right breath

Mars, the Sun, Saturn and Rāhu. Good for eating, love, battle.

Its source. Śiva learned it from Viṣṇu and taught it to Gaurī. 72,000 channels rise from below the navel; three are chief.

FIVE ELEMENTS IN A BREATH

Reading the flow in the nostril
Firebreath along the upper edge
Waterbreath along the lower edge
Winda slanting flow
Earthstraight through the middle
Etherfilling the whole nostril

Balance. When both nostrils flow equally, no rite should be started: the text calls it the equator of life.

Ether’s use. Each element suits a kind of act; ether alone suits penance for one’s own release.

ZAPPY: Yoga students still check which nostril is open.

PROF. BOLT: This is an early written form of that svara practice.

GRAMMAR AND METRE

Chapters 205–212

🔤 Grammar

A short digest of Sanskrit grammar: sounds, word-forms, compounds.

🎼 Prosody

The metres of verse, from the Vēdic to the classical.

📚 Why here

Without grammar and metre, mantras and verses cannot be said correctly; they are aids to scripture itself.

🔗 Shared

The Agni Purāṇa carries similar chapters; both act as general handbooks.

ZAPPY: A Purāṇa teaching grammar?

PROF. BOLT: It teaches everything needed to use the other chapters. Grammar is the first tool.

WHAT IT TEACHES

A world full of signs

🌍 Order

Earth, sky and body follow one pattern.

🔭 Attention

Watch the stars, the road, the breath: nothing is meaningless.

⚖️ Karma land

Bhārata is where effort counts; signs advise, they do not decide.

📜 A record

Much is folk belief, preserved as it was held.

ZAPPY: One line?

PROF. BOLT: The world speaks in signs; the Purāṇa wrote down how people listened.
